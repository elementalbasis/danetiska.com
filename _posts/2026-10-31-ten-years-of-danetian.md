Danetian has been in development for nearly ten years. During this time, it has undergone numerous revisions, some minor and others so extensive that the resulting language would have been scarcely recognizable to a speaker of an earlier version. Fortunately, I have preserved much of this history in dictionaries, grammatical sketches, translations, and miscellaneous notes, some dating back to my first experiments with Proto-Indo-European.

What follows is a timeline of the language’s external history, from those early experiments to the modern written standard. Unlike its fictional history, which spans several thousand years, this is the history of Danetian as it was actually written and revised.
