**Javier Castro** is the creator of the Danetian language, and founder of the
Danetian Academy. His fascination with languages began in childhood, and soon
led him to create languages of his own, an interest which would eventually
lead him to start the Danetian project.

Danetian brings together several of his interest: history, linguistics,
literature, and programming, among others. His work extends across the project,
from individual words and grammatical forms, to translations, reference works,
and software. He maintains the dictionary and corpus, the language's grammatical
description, and develops tools for processing lexical entries and generating
inflections. This combination of linguistics and technical work allows him to
advance his goal of making Danetian a language that can be read, written,
studied, and used in all aspects of life.

The Royal Spanish Academy has been an important influence on his conception of
the Danetian Academy, particularly in its lexicography, scholarship, and
editorial presentation. Through the Danetian Academy, he seeks to give the
project a coherent institutional home, and bring contributors to its continual
development.
