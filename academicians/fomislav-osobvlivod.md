A historian, philologist, and lifelong enthusiast of languages and
worldbuilding, **Fomislav Nicolajivić Osobvlivod** has been creating
constructed languages since 2010. Originally trained in engineering, he later
pursued his interests in history and linguistics, eventually becoming a teacher
of History and English at a higher technical college.

His interests include Slavic languages, historical linguistics, and the
relationship between language, history, and culture. Over the years, he has
developed numerous constructed languages and fictional settings, drawing on his
academic background and personal research.

His principal project is **Thomalonia**, an extensive fictional world that
brings together his interests in linguistics, history, and worldbuilding. He
continues to develop the project alongside his teaching career, treating it as
both a creative undertaking and an opportunity to explore new subjects.

He has also contributed to the development of the Danetian language, offering
valuable advice and a critical perspective on several important questions,
particularly those concerning its orthography, linguistic conventions, and
historical development. His knowledge of linguistics and philology has made him
a trusted interlocutor in discussions that have helped shape and refine the
language.
