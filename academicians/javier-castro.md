**Javier Castro** is the creator of the Danetian language. He has been
fascinated with languages since a young age, an interest which would eventually
lead him to create his own.

The Danetian language brings together several of his interest: history,
linguistics, literature, and programming, among others. As the
principal maintainer of the project, his work has involved coining new words,
translating texts, creating reference documents, and writing accompanying
software. This combination of linguistics and
technical work allows him to advance his goal of making Danetian a language
that can be read, written, studied, and used in all aspects of life.
Through the Danetian Academy, he seeks to give the
language a coherent institutional home, and bring contributors to its continual
development.
